Privacy Policy for Bulk Balance

Effective Date: 5 June 2026

Bulk Balance is a calorie and protein tracking application.

Data Collection

Bulk Balance stores food, calorie, protein and meal information entered by users. This information is stored locally on the user's device.

Personal Information

Bulk Balance does not require account creation and does not collect names, addresses, payment information or other personally identifiable information.

Data Sharing

Bulk Balance does not sell, share or transfer user data to third parties.

Data Security

User data is stored locally on the device. Users are responsible for securing access to their device.
